01 / Philosophy

Clarity before complexity.

Great technology leadership is rarely about chasing the newest tool; it is about aligning architecture, governance, and people around a shared mission.

With a Master of Public Administration (MPA) and executive technology leadership across scientific and association ecosystems, I approach digital transformation through the lens of institutional stewardship. Every platform decision should reduce friction for staff, elevate the member experience, and withstand scrutiny over time.

Today, my focus sits at the intersection of modern enterprise architecture and practical AI adoption—helping organizations move past siloed experimentation into clear, responsible-use frameworks that amplify human judgment rather than replacing it.
